# Focus Check — Light Table plugin (API 1)

Unzip the plugin distribution, then choose **Settings → Plugins → Install or update plugin…** in Light Table and select the `.ltplugin` folder itself. Open installed tools from the **Plugins** menu or Settings. Plugins are optional and run in their own sandboxed process. Packages are ad-hoc signed for Apple Silicon and macOS 14+, not notarized, so macOS may ask for explicit local approval.

- **Focus Check:** select photos in Light Table, open the plugin, and choose **Find & Mark**. Turn on **Check all photos in current folder** to check the folder instead. A photo is marked when it missed focus, for one of three reasons shown beside it in the results: **nothing in the frame is sharp**; **it is blurry where the camera focused** — nothing is sharp around the Canon focus points (the AF zone you selected and the points that locked, with a margin), meaning focus landed somewhere else; or **it is soft at the focus point** — the subject right under the locked points (a jersey, numbers, a helmet) is soft, even if a crowd or another player nearby is sharp. Photos without Canon focus-point data get only the first test. Heavy motion blur, which keeps some crisp edges, is usually not marked.
- **Mark by:** **Mark for deletion** by default, a **color label**, a **1-star rating** (in place of any rating the photo had), or **untagging**. Marking for deletion never deletes the photo. Filter by the deletion status in Light Table, select the marked photos, and use Move to Trash when ready. Other marking methods change only their own value.
- **Protect your picks:** **Skip tagged photos** (on at first) and **Skip photos rated ★… or more** (off at first) keep a soft pick from ever being marked. Protected photos are listed under **Left alone**. A photo the camera protected counts as tagged, as it does in Light Table. Untagging changes only tagged photos, so it doesn't use Skip tagged photos; protect your best frames by rating instead.
- **100% review:** click a marked photo to see it at 100% (one photo pixel per screen pixel, like Light Table's 100% zoom) around the focus points that locked (red; the other selected points are white), with the whole frame below it. Drag the photo, or click the small frame, to look around; **←** and **→** step through the results, and Esc goes back to them. **Keep** (or **K**) gives that one photo back its previous deletion mark, label, rating, or tag and takes it off the list. RAW files whose embedded preview is smaller than the photo (for example many Sony files) take a few seconds to open at full size.
- **Undo** puts back every marked photo's previous deletion mark, label, rating, or tag, and **Close** (or Esc) returns to Light Table. **Close & Hide Deletion Marks** returns and filters those photos out. Focus results count photos that passed, missed focus, could not be judged, or could not be read. After marking, Focus Check reads the values back from the files and reports any Light Table didn't save. RAW files are checked from their embedded full-size preview. Photos are read only; marks are written through Light Table.
